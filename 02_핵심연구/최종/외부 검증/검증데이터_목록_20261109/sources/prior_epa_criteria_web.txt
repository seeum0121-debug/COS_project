L213@P11: 1. INTRODUCTION
L214@P11: This report serves as an addendum to the NIEHS report, “Prospective and
L215@P11: Retrospective Evaluation of the Eye Irritation Potential of Agrochemical
L216@P11: Formulations” (Choksi et al., 2021) describing the two-phased evaluation of
L217@P11: agrochemical formulations conducted previously. Herein we describe a third phase of
L218@P11: prospective studies conducted to evaluate the usefulness and limitations of a group of
L219@P11: in vitro test methods1 that could potentially be combined into a defined approach to
L220@P11: assign hazard classification and personal protective equipment (PPE) labeling for eye
L221@P11: irritation potential based on the United Nations Globally Harmonized System for
L222@P11: Classification and Labeling of Chemicals (GHS) and U.S. Environmental Protection
L223@P11: Agency (EPA) hazard classification systems (EPA, 2004; United Nations, 2019;
L224@P11: Table 1). While the scope of this report is focused on the methods and results
L225@P11: associated with Phase 3 prospective testing, we have also included results from
L226@P11: Phases 1 and 2 to provide a comprehensive view of the full study results in one place.
L227@P11: By doing so, we can provide the results used for analyses and interpretation of the full
L228@P11: data set generated throughout all phases of this study. Given the recognized
L229@P11: limitations of the in vivo test method (Clippinger et al., 2021; Luechtefeld et al.,
L230@P11: 2016), particularly with respect to mild and moderate irritants, we have not included
L231@P11: concordance analyses herein but instead provided results that can be assessed across
L232@P11: all assays included in all three study phases.

L399@P14: 2.1 Phase 3 Goals

L401@P14: dataset of results from multiple in vitro eye irritation test methods for a
L402@P14: reference list of agrochemicals. These results will be used as a proof-ofprinciple to determine whether these method(s) might be useful in defined
L403@P14: approaches to determine the eye irritation potential of agrochemical
L404@P14: formulations.

L408@P14: one of the two classification systems, were tested in EO and BCOP (n=5 EPA
L409@P14: II/GHS 2A; n=1 EPA II/GHS NC; n=3 EPA III/GHS 2B; n=4 EPA III/GHS
L410@P14: NC).
