L2416@P76: E. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
L2417@P76: PPE requirements are specified for uses covered under the Worker Protection Standard
L2418@P76: (WPS), but there are no regulatory requirements for non-WPS products, products used by
L2419@P76: residents, or products intended only for manufacturing use. However, to protect human
L2420@P76: health, the following guidance is offered.
L2421@P76: 1. For Non-WPS (Industrial/Commercial) Uses. While there are no regulatory
L2422@P76: requirements that demand PPE for non-WPS products, many states test applicators for
L2423@P76: their comprehension regarding what types of PPE are used and how to use it correctly,
L2424@P76: however, this training and testing is normally only done for users of RUPs, not
L2425@P76: unclassified pesticides. Label reviewers should ensure that adequate, understandable
L2426@P76: language regarding the types of PPE that should be worn for the product’s hazards is
L2427@P76: included in any label, whether RUP or unclassified. In cases where the reviewers
L2428@P76: determine PPE would be necessary, the various PPE tables in Chapter 10 provide
L2429@P76: information about which PPE is protective in specific circumstances. If there is an
L2430@P76: applicable regulatory document which specifies PPE requirements based on concerns
L2431@P76: specific to the active ingredient then those PPE requirements must be placed on the
L2432@P76: label.
L2433@P76: 2. For Products used by Residents/Consumers. In order to protect human health, label
L2434@P76: reviewers should review the toxicity data and the product’s uses to determine whether
L2435@P76: PPE would be necessary to meet the standards for registration. In cases where the
L2436@P76: reviewers determine PPE would be necessary, the PPE tables in Chapter 10 provide
L2437@P76: information about which PPE is protective in specific circumstances. In some cases, the
L2438@P76: PPE indicated in these tables may need to be modified; for example, to fit the
L2439@P76: consumer’s ability to acquire it. For example, “shoes” may need to be substituted for
L2440@P76: “chemical resistant footwear” or “safety glasses” may need to be substituted for
L2441@P76: “protective eyewear”. If there is an applicable regulatory document which specifies PPE
L2442@P76: requirements based on concerns specific to the active ingredient then those PPE
L2443@P76-77: requirements must be placed on the label. Label Review Manual
